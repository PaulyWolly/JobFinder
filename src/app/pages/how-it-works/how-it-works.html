How JobFinder Works

Find live roles, move the ones you want into Applications, apply on the real posting, then tell JobFinder whether you sent it. You can send a role back to Jobs at any time.

  1. 1

    Set what you want

    Start on Profile and Search Settings. Titles, skills, locations, salary, and work type drive the live search. The Dashboard uses those preferences for Found, Screened, and Matched counts.

  2. 2

    Find jobs

    Open Jobs to see listings from boards such as Himalayas, The Muse, ZipRecruiter, and others. Filter by remote or on-site, full-time or contract, US, and job site. Refresh after you change Search Settings.

  3. 3

    Move keepers to Applications

    Click Save to Applications on a role you want to pursue. That listing leaves Jobs and shows up under Action required. It is a move, not a copy. Use Remove on Jobs only if you never want to see that listing again.

  4. 4

    Apply on the real posting

    Go to Applications and click Apply. JobFinder opens the employer or board page in a new tab so you can submit there. We do not fill the application for you.

  5. 5

    Confirm when you return

    When you come back to Applications, a prompt asks if you applied. Choose Yes, I applied to move the role to the Applied tab and update the Applied count. Choose Not yet to leave it in Action required so you can try again.

  6. 6

    Pass without losing the listing

    If you decide not to move forward, click Return to Jobs on the card or in the prompt. The role leaves Applications and shows up on Jobs again so you can save it later.
