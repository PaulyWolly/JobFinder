What to do

Work top to bottom. You can send a role back to Jobs at any time if you do not want to move forward.

  1. 1 Find jobs

    Open Jobs and review live listings from the boards.
  2. 2 Move keepers

    Save a role to Applications. It leaves Jobs until you send it back.
  3. 3 Apply

    On Applications, click Apply to open the real posting.
  4. 4 Confirm

    When you return, say whether you applied. That updates Applied.

  5. 5 Or pass

    Return to Jobs if you do not want to pursue it. Remove hides it for good.
